This repository covers Pico-assisted boot into Fastboot with backups of previously documented regions, temporary root over regular USB, verified feature modifications, and the Powerful6700 app. "Read-only" describes the goal of a feature or backup; it does not mean that temporary files, booting, or USB mode switching have no side effects at all on the device.
